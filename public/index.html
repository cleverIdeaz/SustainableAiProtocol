The Problem

AI's energy consumption is invisible. We can't manage what we don't measure.

No Standard

Every AI provider uses different metrics, estimates, or reports nothing at all. There's no universal way to measure energy use across models and platforms.

No Transparency

Users have no idea how much energy their AI interactions consume. It's completely hidden from view, making accountability impossible.

No Data

Without real-world measurement data, we can't optimize, can't improve efficiency, and can't make informed decisions about AI usage.

No Button

The simplest solution—a universal tracking button—doesn't exist. Every AI chat should have one. None do.

Our Approach

We're building open infrastructure for AI energy measurement. Starting with the button. Building credibility through transparency.

Scientific Validation

What This Actually Is (Right Now)

SAP is currently a beta tracking system with a button demo. The energy calculations are rough estimates based on industry averages, not scientifically validated measurements. The button works—you can integrate it today—but the data needs validation.

We're not claiming to solve AI sustainability. We're building the basic measurement tool (the button) that should exist in every AI chat interface. The concept is simple. The execution requires validation, research partnerships, and real-world testing.

For Developers

Integrate the SAP button into your AI applications. Help us collect real-world data.

One-Line Integration 
<!-- Add SAP button to any AI interface -->
<script src="https://sustainableaiprotocol.com/sap-embed.js"></script>

<!-- The button auto-appears and spins on AI interactions -->
<!-- Track custom interactions manually -->
<script>
  SAP.track({
    model: 'gpt-4',
    tokens: 150,
    type: 'text'
  });
</script>
