The Problem

AI's energy consumption is invisible. We can't manage what we don't measure.

No Standard

Every AI provider uses different metrics, estimates, or reports nothing at all. There's no universal way to measure energy use across models and platforms.

No Transparency

Users have no idea how much energy their AI interactions consume. It's completely hidden from view, making accountability impossible.

No Data

Without real-world measurement data, we can't optimize, can't improve efficiency, and can't make informed decisions about AI usage.

No Accountability

AI companies can claim sustainability without proof. Users can't make informed choices. The environmental cost remains invisible.

Our Approach

We're building open infrastructure for AI energy measurement. Starting simple, validating everything, building credibility.

📊

Open Tracking Protocol

Free SDK that any developer can integrate to track AI interactions. Open source, transparent methodology.

🔬

Scientific Validation

Partner with researchers to validate energy calculations. Publish methodology. Build credibility through peer review.

🌐

Crowd-Sourced Data

Aggregate anonymous data from all integrations. Build the largest dataset of real AI energy usage.

What This Actually Is (Right Now)

SAP is currently a beta tracking system that estimates AI energy usage based on rough industry averages. The calculations are not scientifically validated. The data is not comprehensive. This is early stage infrastructure that needs validation, research partnerships, and real-world testing.

We're not claiming to solve AI sustainability. We're trying to build the basic measurement tools that don't exist yet. That starts with honest data collection and transparent methodology.
