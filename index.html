01 / What sustainability means

Intelligence should sustain
what makes it possible.

Just as sap moves resources through a living tree, Sustainable AI Protocol aims to connect the signals and choices that keep a growing AI ecosystem viable.

◉

People

Protect human agency, dignity, privacy, opportunity, and the ability to make meaningful decisions alongside AI.

✳

Planet

Bring energy, emissions, water, hardware use, and ecological consequences into view—without pretending estimates are measurements.

∞

Prosperity

Favor durable value over waste: task quality, affordability, access, efficiency, and responsible use of shared resources.

Evidence is the connective tissue.

SAP should preserve where a claim came from, what was measured versus estimated, and what remains unknown. Human rights and dignity are not reducible to a single impact score.

03 / See the idea in action

Invisible by default.
Transparent on demand.

The current SAP Lab is a working, browser-local comparison prototype. Try Balanced, Eco, and Performance; require local-only processing; and inspect why a hypothetical execution path was chosen.

Important: The lab uses fictional figures and runs no AI inference. It demonstrates decision logic, not real-world energy savings or platform-wide enforcement.

04 / An open invitation to builders

A protocol earns adoption by being useful.

SAP v0.2 is a reference implementation, not a ratified standard. Developers can run the comparison core today and help test whether a shared cross-system contract adds something existing standards don't already provide.

  • Use what already exists. Connect to established tools for model access, telemetry, carbon methodology, and verification rather than cloning them.
  • Make claims inspectable. Carry measurement quality and uncertainty alongside every metric.
  • Keep people in charge. No mandatory centralized tracking, moral scoring, or invisible policy overrides.
  • Next milestones: real measured pilot, adapter experiments, independently tested benefits.

sap-core.js / experimental API
// No account, remote service, or model required.
const SAP = require("./sap-core");

const plan = SAP.evaluate(options, {
  mode: "balanced",
  minimumQuality: 0.80,
  localOnly: true
});

// The host decides whether and how to execute.
// A plan is not proof of actual energy saved.
console.log(plan.selected, plan.reason);
